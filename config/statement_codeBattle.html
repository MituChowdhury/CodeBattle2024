Rule Book

The Goal

Reach oppenents Base as many times as possible.

Map:

The game is played on a map of size 22x17, the origin being at the top left corner.
The map itself is a grid of 17 x 22. Each block is called a 'Tile'.
Every tile is divided into a 10 x 10 grid where every unit is called 'Subtile'.

Minions

  • Minions are the main character of the game. They have a player owning them, a location on the map, movement speed, hitpoints.
  • There will be at most 5 minions at a time on the map of a player. A new minion will spawn everytime one is killed. They will spawn at both ends of the map, player 1 on the left, player 2 on the right.
  • Each turn they move towards the base of their opponent by their movement speed in the shortest possible path. When their opponent kills them opponent gets points.

    • Spawn place:

      Minions will spawn at both ends approaching towards the other side. Players will choose the Y coordinate of the spawning place(ex: The flags in the previous image).
      Your task is to stop the opponent attackers from reaching your side and to support your own attackers.
      Each player starts with 350 money and has 20 lives, losing one for each attacker reaching the own base.

    • Location:

      The Tile where the minion is standing currently consist of x and y.
      x,y represents the possition in respect to width and height.

    • Speed:

      The maximum number of subtiles a minion can cross in a single turn of the game. All the minions have the same speed
      Initially the speed is 10 which means a minion can walk through at most 10 subtiles.

    • Hitpoints

      Unit of measurement representing a character's or creature's health or life. Hitpoints indicate how much damage a character or entity can withstand before being defeated or killed.

Towers :

  • A tower has an owner, a location, damage, range and cooldown time. When a tower has an attacker in range and a cooldown time of 0, the tower will shoot at the attacker and be inactive for the next few turns, depending on the reload time. An attacker is in range of a tower, when the pythagorean distance between them is less than or equal to the tower range.

  • Building place of a tower:

    Towers can be built on any tile of the map. It is not possible to build a tower at the same cell as another tower.

  • Cooldown time

    denotes the minimun number of time a tower needs to reload and attack again.

  • Types of Towers:

    A player can build different types of towers as follows:

    • Gun tower

      Attacks one minion at a time, reducing the hitpoint by its damage value. In case of multiple possible targets, it will always take the one closest to the own base. Once the attack begins it will keep attacking the same enemy character until the object dies or gets destroyed or goes out of its range.

    • Fire tower

      The fire tower damages all opponent attackers in its range. The attack will last 2 turns, damaging the units that are in range during the turn of the attack happening or one turn after.

    • Stun tower

      The stun tower does not cause any damage to the attackers. Instead it will slow them down for some turns. It targets all attackers in range, ignoring those which are already slowed down.

Bomb

It exploads when a minion steps on it and applies damage to all the minions on it's range. Only the enemy minions gets the damage.

Spring trap:

A spring trap can be of 4 types.

  • Spring North:

    If any character steps on the tile , it flies 5 tiles toward north directions.

  • Spring South:

    If any character steps on the tile , it flies 5 tiles toward south directions.

  • Spring East:

    If any character steps on the tile , it flies 5 tiles toward East directions.

  • Spring West:

    If any character steps on the tile , it flies 5 tiles toward West directions.

Turn:

the game is played in turns. In each turn the outputs of the players code is used to instruct the minions.
Initially the number of turns is set to 20.
The turn order is the following:
  • Build and Attack commands are handled
  • Towers shoot and kill minions
  • Alive minions move
  • Minions, which reached the opponent's base, disappear and and a new minion is spawned
  • After all the turn is completed winner announced.

Victory condition:

  • Initially:

    The player reaching the opponent's base maximum number of times wins.

  • In case of tie:

    Player having the most coins left wins.

  • In case of tie:

    Player whose characters got killed less times wins

    • Otherwise the game is called a Tie.

Game Input

  • Initial Input

    • Line 1: playerId, either 0 (you defend the left side) or 1 (you defend the right side)
    • Line 2: width w & height h, the width and height of the map
    • Next, h lines will be printed each line containing w characters of either ‘.’ or ‘#’. Where ‘.’ denotes no obstacle and ‘#’ denotes an indestructible obstacle.

  • Input in Each turn

    • First line will contain two integers, player_money and opponent_money , denoting remaining money of the player and remaining money of the opponent
    • Next line has two integer sc_p and sc_o denoting the score of the player and the score of the opponent
    • The next five lines contains 5 space separated integers {id, x, y, health, speed} describing the Id of the character, x and y coordinates of the player, health and status of the characters of the player.

Expected Output

  • Initial output:

    The player needs to print 5 integers, separated by spaces. These integers will be the y coordinates (row position) of the spawn positions. The characters will always spawn at these y-coordinates in case it gets killed or reaches the opponent base. The spawn x-coordinate is 0 for player 1 and Width-1 for player 2.

  • Turn Output :

    In each turn the player must print 5 lines for each of their 5 characters. Each line must contain one of the following 3 commands containing your actions, separated by a ;
    an action can be:
    • GO Player_Id to move forward
    • BUIlD Tower_type Player_Id to build a tower at current tiletype can be GUNTOWER, FIRETOWER or GLUETOWER
    • ATTACK Player_Id to attack the nearest tower/wall

  • Invalid outputs:

    • The player does not maintain the structure of the 3 commands mentioned above.
    • A character tries to build something at a position where there is already an object. (Exception is while building a bomb)
    • A character tries to build an object at his current position.
    • A character tries to build something outside the map.
    • A character can only choose one of his 8 neighbour tiles to build something. Exception: A character can build bombs if the pythagorean distance between his current position and the target position is less than 3.

      • In Case of invalid outputs the player's code will be terminated immediatly and the player will lose.

Constraints

... contains between 1 and 500 characters.

Allotted response time for per turn is ≤ 50 ms (1000 ms for the first turn).
