The Goal

Reach oppenents Base as many times as possible.

  Rules

The game is played on a map of size 22x17, the origin being in the top left corner.
The map itself is a grid of 17 x 22. Each block is called a 'Tile'.
Every tile is divided into a 10 x 10 grid where every unit is called 'Subtile'.

Spawn place:

Attackers will spawn both ends(ex: The flags in the previous image.), approaching towards the other side.
Your task is to stop the opponent attackers from reaching your side and to support your own attackers.
Each player starts with 350 money and has 20 lives, losing one for each attacker reaching the own base.

Minions

Minions have a player owning them, a location on the map, movement speed, hitpoints.
There will be at most 5 minions at a time on the map. A new minion will spawn everytime one is killed. They will spawn at both ends of the map symmetrically, those attacking player 1 on the left, player 2 on the right. Each turn they move towards the base of their opponent by their movement speed in the shortest possible path.
When their opponent kills them opponent gets points.

Location:

The Tile where the minion is standing currently consist of x and y.
x,y represents the possition in respect to width and height.

Speed:

The maximum number of subtiles a minion can cross in a single turn of the game.
Initially the speed is 10 which means a minion can walk through at most 10 subtiles.

Hitpoints

unit of measurement representing a character's or creature's health or life. Hitpoints indicate how much damage a character or entity can withstand before being defeated or killed.

Towers

A tower has an owner, a location, damage, range, reload and cooldown time. When a tower has an attacker in range and a cooldown time of 0, the tower will shoot at the attacker and be inactive for the next few turns, depending on the reload time.
An attacker is in range of a tower, when the pythagorean distance between them is less than or equal to the tower range.

Build tower:

Towers can be built on any tile of the map. It is not possible to build a tower at the same cell as another tower.

Cool-down time

denotes the minimum number of time a tower needs to reload and attack again.

Types of Towers

A player can build different types of towers as follows:
Gun tower
The gun tower attacks one minion at a time, reducing the hit point by its damage value. In case of multiple possible targets, it will always take the one closest to the own base.
Fire tower
The fire tower damages all opponent attackers in its range. The attack will last 2 turns, damaging the units that are in range during the turn of the attack happening or one turn after.
Stun tower
The stun tower does not cause any damage to the attackers. Instead, it will slow them down for some turns. It targets all attackers in range, ignoring those which are already slowed down.

Turn:

the game is played in turns. In each turn the outputs of the players code is used to instruct the minions.
Initially the number of turns is set to 20.
The turn order is the following:
  • Build commands are handled
  • Towers shoot and kill attackers
  • Alive attackers move
  • Attackers, which reached the opponent base, disappear and reduce the player lives
  • The player reaching the opponent’s base maximum number of times wins
  • The player reaching the opponent’s base maximum number of times wins

Game Input

Initial Input

Line 1: playerId, either 0 (you defend the left side) or 1 (you defend the right side)

Line 2: width w & height h, the width and height of the map

Next, h lines will be printed each line containing w characters of either ‘.’ or ‘#’. Where ‘.’ denotes no obstacle and ‘#’ denotes an indestructible obstacle.

Input in Each turn

First line will contain two integers, player_money and opponent_money , denoting remaining money of the player and remaining money of the opponent

Next line has two integer sc_p and sc_o denoting the score of the player and the score of the opponent

The next five lines contains 5 space separated integers {id, x, y, health, speed} describing the Id of the character, x and y coordinates of the player, health and status of the characters of the player.

  Expected Output

   Initial output:

The player needs to print 5 integers, separated by spaces. These integers will be the y coordinates (row position) of the spawn positions. The characters will always spawn at these y-coordinates in case it gets killed or reaches the opponent base. The spawn x-coordinate is 0 for player 1 and Width-1 for player 2.
Turn Output :
In each turn the player must print 5 lines for each of their 5 characters. Each line must contain one of the following 3 commands containing your actions, separated by a ;
an action can be:
  • GO Player_Id to move forward
  • BUIlD Tower_type Player_Id to build a tower at current tiletype can be GUNTOWER, FIRETOWER or GLUETOWER
  • ATTACK Player_Id to attack the nearest tower/wall

Constraints
... contains between 1 and 500 characters.

Allotted response time for per turn is ≤ 50 ms (1000 ms for the first turn).
